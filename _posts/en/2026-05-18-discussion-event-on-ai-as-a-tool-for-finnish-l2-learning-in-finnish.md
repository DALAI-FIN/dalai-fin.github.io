Dalai-FIN started the codevelopment of the Finnish L2 AI Assistant Ecosystem by ordering a qualitative study on the views of Finnish L2 teachers regarding AI and its use in language teaching and learning.

In our seminar we will present the results of the interview, and give a short demonstration of the current AI assistant developed by DALAI-FIN. 

The event is hybrid: live at University of Helsinki (Fabianinkatu 24A) 28.5.2026 klo 13:00-15:30 and online. Please enroll via this link:
[https://redcap.link/S2-opetus-ja-tekoaly-28-5-26](https://redcap.link/S2-opetus-ja-tekoaly-28-5-26)

The event is held in Finnish.
